The competition

Approachable to start. Open-ended enough to surprise.

Unibots is designed for students with any level of robotics experience, leaving plenty of room for creative and ambitious engineering.

  • Build a compact, fully autonomous robot as a team
  • Earn points in head-to-head-to-head-to-head matches
  • Balance mechanical design, electronics, software, and match strategy
  • Learn from other teams and improve between events

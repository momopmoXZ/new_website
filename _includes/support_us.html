Why support Unibots?

Invest in practical engineering talent

Unibots is an inter-university robotics competition designed to be accessible, energetic, and genuinely hands-on. Built around challenge, creativity, and community, it asks students to solve real engineering problems with simple machines they design, build, debug, and compete with themselves.

Reach motivated students

Meet engineers, computer scientists, designers, and builders while they are actively applying their skills.

Enable accessible robotics

Help keep entry and attendance free while supporting the equipment and infrastructure students need.

Build meaningful connections

Share your work through talks, event presence, volunteering, and conversations with teams.

Support with your time

Help make the event happen

Not every contribution has to be financial. Unibots events rely on volunteers to help teams feel welcome, keep matches moving, and create the community that makes students want to keep building.

Support the event

Help with setup, team check-in, schedules, match flow, and the practical details that keep the day flowing!

Support the teams

Keep the energy high, answer questions, and help create a friendly competition atmosphere!

Share expertise

Join judging, give technical feedback, or bring industry perspective to students building their first robots!

Whether you can give a day, bring a colleague group, or help at a specific regional event, your time directly makes Unibots possible.
